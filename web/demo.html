 TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

Gemini proposes.
The verifier decides.

We sample the waiting area every 0.5 metres, at 1.2 metres above the pavement. At 15-minute intervals, rays follow the computed sun direction through buildings, trees, glass and the proposed canopy.

  1. At least +25 percentage points of daily shade.
  2. At least 40% shade at the worst time in the peak-heat window.
  3. Inside the buildable area, within the £8,000 budget, with geometry and clearance checks.

This is direct-sun shading, not air temperature or UV protection. OSM building heights and foliage can be estimates. Passing the geometric checks is not structural or planning approval. The recorded attempt history used the original full-day gate; the final design is rechecked against the current rules.
